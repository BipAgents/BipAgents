# BipAgents' name and icon

Everything in this repository is under the [MIT license](LICENSE): the code, and also the Bips (their drawings, the
pixel engine and its data, their babble, their voices and samples, the recordings, the screenshots). Third-party pieces
keep their own licences, listed where they are used. Two things are not covered by the MIT license, because they are
the project's marks: its name and its icon.

## The marks

- The name **BipAgents** (also written **Bip Agents**).
- The app's icon: `App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`, `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/`,
  `android/app/src/main/res/values/ic_launcher_background.xml`, and what `scripts/pixel/icon.py` draws.

## What you may do

- Say that a product is built on BipAgents, uses it, or is compatible with it.
- Redistribute this project unmodified, under its name.
- Make a fork, changed as you like, for your own use or to contribute back.
- Write about BipAgents and show it (articles, reviews, videos, talks).

## What you may not do

- Publish an app, a service or a product named BipAgents, or with a name close enough to be confused with it.
- Use the icon, or one close enough to be confused with it, for anything other than this project.
- Suggest that Kerfy Studio, BipAgents' publisher, made, endorses or supports what you publish when it does not.

A fork you distribute (on a store, as a service, as downloads) needs its own name and its own icon. It may keep the
Bips, their voices and everything else: they are MIT.
